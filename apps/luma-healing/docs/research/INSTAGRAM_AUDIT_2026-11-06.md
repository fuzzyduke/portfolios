## Brand signals

1. **Core promise:** a journey back to yourself; self-listening, self-understanding, self-compassion, not a cure or quick transformation.
2. **Audience language:** women or adults balancing roles such as mother, partner, daughter, professional; guilt, boundaries, people-pleasing, second-guessing, emotional disconnection. Audience gender is an inference, not a confirmed targeting decision.
3. **Offer surface:** Reiki and Akashic Records are explicitly named, alongside self-awareness, emotional patterns and nervous-system awareness. The images do not specify session formats or qualifications.
4. **Visual system:** warm ivory/cream, muted sage-green botanical foliage, lavender circular brushstroke/soft watercolor, subdued blue-grey typography, occasional warm yellow flowers, scenic water/sky/mountains, serif headline plus script accent.
5. **Tone:** gentle, invitational, reassuring, question-led, non-shaming. Educational carousels are a key content format. Avoid claims that Reiki or Akashic Records diagnose or treat illness; avoid framing psychological symptoms as verified clinical outcomes.
6. **Engagement techniques:** save prompts, reflection questions, comment invites. This content can inform a journal/reflection section on the website.

## Proposed information architecture (not implemented)

1. Hero: personal promise and practitioner identity, keeping the existing calm palette.
2. Meet Reshma: story, motivation, training, photo and boundaries of practice, using verified details.
3. Explore sessions: Reiki and Akashic Records only if Reshma confirms both as bookable offerings; clear distinctions and expectations.
4. What a session involves: actual duration, format, location/remote availability and preparation.
5. Reflection/journal: curated educational themes based on the carousels, with permission for reused creative assets.
6. FAQs and transparent disclaimers; then one clear, functional contact/booking CTA.
