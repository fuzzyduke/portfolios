# PRODUCT.md — Luma Healing Space Microsite

## Purpose
The primary objective of the **Luma Healing Space** microsite is to present a gentle, calming, and visually restorative web experience that Reshma can send to prospective clients over WhatsApp or social media.

## Target Audience
- Adults seeking relaxation, emotional grounding, quiet reflection, Reiki, and restorative energy practices.
- Visitors are strictly framed as individuals seeking self-care, **never** as medical patients.

## Primary Conversion
- **WhatsApp Conversation Handoff**: Every CTA routes the user directly to a pre-filled WhatsApp message customized by session type (e.g., Reiki Session, Distance Reiki, Energy Healing, Guided Relaxation).

## Key Positioning & Aesthetics
- **Primary Line**: *A gentle space for Reiki, energy healing and restorative wellbeing.*
- **Supporting Line**: *Slow down. Reconnect. Restore.*
- **Visual Design**: Soft lavender, muted sage/green, warm gold, cream off-white, and eclipse glow energy portal motifs.
