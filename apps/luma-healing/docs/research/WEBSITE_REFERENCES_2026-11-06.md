## Prioritized design direction

For V2: blend Soul Serenity's visual restraint, omspirit's human voice, Reiki Discovery's clear practitioner/booking path, and Sacred Echos' service-explainer format. Preserve Luma's lavender/sage/gold/cream palette.

Later: emulate HealSana's resource lead magnet and AkashicRecords.com's educational library only after services, pricing and demand are validated. Workshops and memberships are speculative.

## Proposed V2 page outline

1. Hero with a practitioner-led promise (use “A journey back to yourself” if approved by Reshma).
2. Personal introduction, authentic portrait and story.
3. Verified bookable services, each with format, duration, price and CTA.
4. What to expect, FAQ and appropriate disclaimer.
5. Selected reflection excerpts from Instagram, with permission.
6. Inquiry/booking and Instagram link.

## Decision gate

Confirm whether Luma Healing Space and Soul Search by Reshma are related identities or a brand transition. Existing Luma CONTENT.md marks Luma as Reshma-verified. No name, claim, contact, pricing or live site changes without review.
