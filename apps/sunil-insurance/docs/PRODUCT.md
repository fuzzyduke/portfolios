# PRODUCT.md — Sunil Insurance Microsite

## Purpose
The primary objective of the **Sunil Insurance** microsite is to generate qualified insurance enquiries for Sunil as an independent human insurance advisor.
It serves as a clean, fast-loading, mobile-first web app that Sunil can easily send to potential customers over WhatsApp.

## Target Customers
1. **Individuals & Families**: Looking for clear health coverage and personal vehicle insurance advice without overwhelming sales pressure.
2. **Senior Citizens & Caregivers**: Seeking guidance on pre-existing condition waiting periods and specialized medical coverage.
3. **Businesses & SMEs**: Looking for group health mediclaim policies, corporate benefits, and commercial vehicle fleet coverage (High-value opportunity).

## Primary Conversion
- **WhatsApp Deep Link Handoff**: Every CTA routes the user directly to a pre-filled WhatsApp message customized by section (e.g. Health, Motor, Corporate, General).

## Key Positioning
- **Central Proposition**: *Insurance made simpler.*
- **Supporting Idea**: *Personal insurance guidance for individuals, families, and businesses.*
- **Core Philosophy**: *"Insurance shouldn't begin with a policy. It should begin with understanding what you actually need."*

## Assumptions
- Static client-side web application deployed at $0/month (Cloudflare Pages).
- No database or authentication required for Phase 1.
- Initial user touchpoint is mobile-heavy (WhatsApp link sharing).
